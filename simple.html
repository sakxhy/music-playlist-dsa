1. What is a Node?

Think of each song card as a SongNode container. It holds the song data (Title & Artist) plus a reference pointer (.next) that connects it to the subsequent song.

2. What is HEAD?

The HEAD pointer is the master bookmark for the start of the playlist. Without HEAD, we cannot find the beginning of our chain in memory.

3. How do Pointers Move?

When you click Next Song, the system simply updates currentSong = currentSong.next. Deleting a song bypasses the node: prev.next = prev.next.next in O(1) pointer updates.
